# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS/JS in a single page, served by GitHub Pages from `master` with the custom domain www.juantobon.dev (CNAME). No build step.

## Users

Recruiters and hiring companies evaluating Juan Tobón for a role as technical lead, DevOps, or applied AI. They arrive from LinkedIn or a CV link, skim in under a minute, and decide whether to reach out.

## Product Purpose

Personal portfolio. Success means a recruiter understands within seconds who Juan is and what level he works at, sees evidence in three real projects, and contacts him through LinkedIn or email.

## Positioning

A technical lead who designs and builds enterprise agent platforms and an agentic software-development harness, and who also owns the DevOps (Kubernetes, GitOps, disaster recovery) those systems run on. Few profiles combine applied AI with production infrastructure ownership.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Language: Spanish only.
- Sections: home (name + role, leaning toward a one-line thesis), projects, stack, contact.
- Contact channels: LinkedIn (juantobon) and email (me@juantobon.dev). No other channels.
- Location shown as "México"; status "Disponible para proyectos".

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Juan Tobón. Monogram "JT" exists in the previous draft.
- The user pinned the palette for the 2026 redesign: matte black and white as the base, with crimson/vermilion and aqua/teal as the colorful accents. Minimalist, playing with transitions and shapes; not boxy.

## Evidence on Hand

Three projects from his current employer (a customs-logistics company). They are private internal systems:
- Plataforma de agentes empresarial (TypeScript, Strands Agents, AWS Bedrock, Claude models except Fable, MCP). Any user with access builds their own agents with team-built tools wired to each business area's APIs and processes, plus their own skills and knowledge bases. Juan built the core and now mainly leads the team that maintains it.
- Harness de desarrollo agéntico: combines agents, skills, and tools for an agentic SDLC on top of corporate tooling (security analysis, best practices, monitoring, deployment, documentation, testing, brand imagery). Juan designs and maintains it for the team.
- DRP con GitOps (CloudFormation, EKS, ArgoCD, Kustomize). Juan defined the DevOps processes and designed the new DRP.

Do not publish metrics, dollar amounts, or the company's name (the user's decision). There are no screenshots, testimonials, or public repos for these projects; do not fabricate any.

## Product Principles

- Evidence over adjectives: projects carry the page, not self-ratings.
- Skimmable in under a minute by a non-technical recruiter.
- Respect confidentiality: describe problem, role, and approach, never numbers or the employer's name.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA contrast, full keyboard navigation, `lang="es"`, and reduced motion honored for all transitions.
